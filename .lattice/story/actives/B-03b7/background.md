## Trigger Event
B-1fa6 accepted evidence that raw detector signals were being treated too much like authoritative defects, while repeated reports lacked a stable canonical identity and historical correction chain. B-fe8f is the required ledger and runtime-evidence prerequisite for this second implementation Story.

## Key Insights
A CandidateFinding is detector output, not gate truth. Canonical defect identity must survive repeated observations without collapsing unrelated defects, and history must preserve corrections through explicit supersession rather than silent overwrite or duplicate advisory noise.

## Solution Tradeoffs
Introduce CandidateFinding, canonicalDefectId, supersession links, historical indexes, recall rules, and non-gate routing receipts on top of the fenced ledger. Conservative matching may leave some duplicates separate, but unsafe merging or allowing detector output to become authority is worse.
