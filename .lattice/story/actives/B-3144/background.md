## Trigger Event
On 2026-07-24, during story B-bf52, a working full-3x2 quorum was destroyed by a redundant quorum_submit re-run after a `git merge main`. The merge changed the whole-tree materialHash but not the storyFilesHash, so the guard in handlers/quorum-submit.ts (raw materialDigest equality only) allowed the resubmit; the escalation floor then drift-accepted the OLD verdicts (eventsWithStoryFilesHashDowngrade rewrote their materialDigest to current) alongside the new ones, and every round collapsed as 'duplicate host/session/key identity'. Recovery required manual surgery on the committed process-events.jsonl (commit 255eefd289) after user approval - there is no supersede or recovery command.

## Key Insights
The root cause is a predicate mismatch between producer and consumer: the quorum_submit duplicate guard's collision predicate (same materialDigest, quorum-submit.ts:485) is strictly narrower than the floor's acceptance predicate (same materialDigest OR storyFilesHash drift-accept, core/review-escalation.ts:948-969). Any commit that moves materialHash without moving storyFilesHash - the normal effect of merging main - falls exactly into the gap between the two predicates. The runIds are deterministic (<storyId>-q-rN, quorum-submit.ts:480,506) with the same derived host/session/key identity per story+host, so both verdict sets land in the same rounds and trip host-independence (core/host-independence.ts:191-205). The naive stale filter (host-independence.ts:154) would have prevented the collision; drift-accept is what defeats it.

## Solution Tradeoffs
(1) Align the guard predicate with the floor (chosen): smallest change, no id-scheme migration, encodes 'a merge that stales passes does not stale a quorum' directly into the tool; refusal message teaches the caller nothing is missing. (2) Material-scoped runIds (<storyId>-q-<digest>-rN): structurally removes collisions but requires migrating existing stories' verdicts and every smoke assertion on the -q-rN format, and redundant resubmits would still silently double-count drift-accepted rounds - rejected for this story, recorded as excluded scope. (3) Supersede events honored by evaluators: heavier trust-model change to an append-only log - rejected. A recovery command (quorum_prune, dry-run + backup) is included because incidents predating the guard fix remain unrecoverable without manual jsonl surgery.
