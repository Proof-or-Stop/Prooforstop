## Key Insights
Because the audit confirmed the surface is safe today, these are hardening measures, not bug fixes. Concretely: the rendered milestone_status/closeout output makes no DONE/Authority/production claim yet carries no explicit disclaimer string; the milestone_status command-policy reason omits "Authority" while milestone_closeout includes it; not_applicable is an operator-set, enum-only self-exemption with no justification requirement (a row can drop a real requirement from the uncovered-milestone check with zero evidence); confirmMilestoneLedger does not re-assert confirmedLedgerHash === ledgerHash at confirm time; three negative controls are missing (duplicate ledger rows, empty / all-not_applicable confirmed ledger, stale-only closeout); and packages/main/tests/milestone-status.test.mjs is not discovered by run-all-smokes (globs dist/tasks/*-smoke.js) so it never runs in the aggregate suite.

## Solution Tradeoffs
Prefer additive, low-blast-radius changes first (disclaimer strings, the Authority reason-string fix, three negative-control tests, and suite wiring) that directly close the audit findings without changing behavior. The two boundary-tightening items (gating not_applicable behind an explicit justification, and re-checking the confirmed-ledger hash at confirm time) must not weaken the existing read-only / PLAN-only contract the audit validated. The milestone-triggered gate-mode default (observe vs enforce) is an explicit recorded decision, not a blind flip, because observe -> enforce is a behavior change with a wide blast radius across every milestone-triggered story.
