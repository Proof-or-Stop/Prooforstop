## Trigger Event
Step 3 of the evidence-integrity program (docs/evidence-integrity-program-spec.md §3), opened 2026-07-26. Root incidents: F-058 (a checklist item, CHK-003 of story B-bf52, was unreachable from the requirement text yet review spun for 12 rounds before anyone noticed, and the story ended NOT DELIVERED) and F-059 (that story's plan.md claimed items delivered from round 4 onward and was never reconciled with the authoritative evidence). The S3 incident and the B-bf52 burn reached 14 finding-bearing rounds / 46 review runs with no effective brake.

## Key Insight
The loss was not the round count itself — it was taking 12 rounds to discover the task was ill-posed. A raw absolute cap would have stopped the spinning but never surfaced the unreachability. The fix is a forced adjudication point: on stall, refuse to mint another ordinary review round until a human records ONE structured action — authorize continuation with a reason + bounded budget, re-plan/split, record a root-cause/architecture escalation, explicitly resolve/disprove/defer the blocking findings or shrink scope, OR declare a checklist item unreachable. The real goal is to surface 'are we converging, or is this task ill-posed?' early and force plan.md/claims to reconcile with evidence at that point.

## Tradeoff
A pure numeric cap is too crude (it cannot tell a converging story from an ill-posed one and would block legitimate progress); a pure advisory (today's behavior) is too weak (it is ignored — 20+ rounds happened). The chosen middle path is a stall-triggered BLOCK that is cleared only by a recorded structured decision, plus a hard upper bound / privileged override so the adjudication cannot itself be ignored indefinitely. The breaker must consider whether blockers are actually decreasing and whether new material genuinely requires another round, not just the raw count. It must extend core/review-convergence.ts and coordinate with core/convergence-breaker.ts (which counts advances, not review rounds) and the story_resume --ack path so the two brakes do not double-count.

## Conversation Texture
The operator wants the review loop to fail loudly and early when a task is ill-posed, instead of silently burning rounds while plan.md drifts from reality. This story delivers the blocking adjudication and the unreachable-item escape hatch that closes the loop; the optional early-warning PreToolUse seatbelt on starting another round is explicitly its own separate follow-on, not part of this story's acceptance.
