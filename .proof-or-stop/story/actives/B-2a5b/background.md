## Key Insights

The frozen protocol supplies N per scale and the budget cap. Reuses lib/instrument.mjs: verifyDir (with a {status:crash} sentinel that maps to a verify_crash stop), sha256, canonicalJson. ## Solution Tradeoffs

The ledger is append-only + identity-keyed (iterationId = <arm>/<scale>/<index>) so a resume (M3) can reconcile by multiset.
