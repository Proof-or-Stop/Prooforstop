## Trigger Event
B-xxxx: internal proof-oriented roadmap review observed that PINN-related research claims can advance with weak evidence, creating overclaim risk across lifecycle stages and inconsistent acceptance semantics.

## Key Insights
Proof-or-Stop already has local claim and readiness mechanics. The gap is not core execution machinery, but controlled extension semantics: PINN workflows need explicit claim-boundary identities and lifecycle gate predicates so stale, mismatched, or unverifiable evidence cannot upgrade claim strength. This story also needs benchmark evidence that the control regime, not task quality, drives reduced unsupported advancement.

## Solution Tradeoffs
We considered a direct scientific-accuracy project and rejected it to avoid overclaim. We also considered a docs-only pass; this is insufficient for validation. Chosen path is implementation-first, with evidence-bound guards and a bounded benchmark harness for A1/A2/A3/A4 control comparisons.

## Conversational Texture
Users wanted a plan to become an executable program with clear milestones, deterministic checks, and explicit public claim boundaries. This story keeps focus on claim admissibility rather than solving PDEs or promising new scientific correctness.
