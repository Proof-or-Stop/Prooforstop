## Key Insights
Current main still separates legacy v1 gate evidence from the v2 chain work, keeps material-driven RED triggers diagnostic-only, and defaults the repro-first intake policy to warning behavior. The next useful deliverable is therefore an honest program plan that separates current truth, target truth, prerequisites, migration, enforcement wiring, and rollout.

## Solution Tradeoffs
Revise the existing repro-first execution plan instead of adding a competing design source. Keep the plan non-authorizing until its prerequisite implementation Stories complete, but make its milestones, evidence contracts, bypass controls, concurrency obligations, and acceptance criteria precise enough to drive later Story creation and review.

## Conversational Texture
The user wants strong enforcement rather than advisory prose, but also wants documentation review to remain practical. The plan must express that distinction precisely: labels do not grant exemptions, executable behavior needs trusted RED and same-target GREEN, pure refactors need characterization, and mechanically proven non-executable scope remains lifecycle-bound without synthetic failures.
