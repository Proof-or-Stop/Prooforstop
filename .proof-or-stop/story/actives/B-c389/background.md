## Key Insights
The two safety invariants underneath M6 and M7 are ALREADY enforced in code and were asserted (not built) by B-8128: candidates never enter active runtime surfacing (matchActivePlaybook guards status===active in core/playbook-runtime-memory.ts), and DONE success never counts as helped (core/learning-store.ts gates outcome counting on !doneSettlement; decisiveSamples excludes done-settlement/neutral/unknown). So this story builds OPERATIONAL SURFACES on top of already-enforced invariants; it must not weaken them. The capability-release ledger + optional lineage fields from B-8128 are the foundation to bind against.

## Solution Tradeoffs
Mirror proven patterns rather than invent: capability_release_backfill mirrors playbook_seed_backfill (default dry-run, explicit --apply, a --digest confirm gate); the exposure surfaces reuse the existing pendingLearning / promotionRecommendations / candidate-inspection rendering; the outcome/promotion commands reuse the existing playbook-outcome + aggregatePlaybookPromotion machinery. The live backfill mutates the git-ignored, host-local playbooks.json, so it must be dry-run-first, idempotent, never activate a candidate, and never infer a helped outcome. Keep candidates inert until evidence-based promotion; promotion requires a concrete exposure plus a structured verdict source.
